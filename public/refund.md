## 1. Overview
ViswaVardhan Vyapara (OPC) Private Limited is committed to providing high-quality digital products, AI services, and consulting solutions. This Cancellations & Refund Policy outlines the terms under which cancellations and refunds are handled for our services and products.

## 2. Cancellation Policy
- **Consulting and Retainer Services**: Clients may cancel recurring retainer agreements by providing at least 30 days written notice prior to the start of the next billing cycle. Fees already billed for the current cycle are non-refundable.
- **Custom Development & Project-Based Work**: Cancellations requested after project kickoff will be billed pro-rata for work completed up to the date of cancellation. Milestone payments already made are non-refundable once milestone deliverables have been provided.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items
The following are strictly non-refundable under all circumstances:
1. Setup fees and onboarding costs
2. Domain registrations and renewals
3. Third-party tool costs, software licences, and API charges
4. Work already delivered and accepted by the client

## 4. Processing Time
Once a refund request has been reviewed and approved, the refund will be processed within 7–10 business days. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
